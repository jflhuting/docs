---

Start by identifying your robot and the configuration that fits your project. Hardware, software, and available resources vary by model.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your platform">
    Browse the [NavBot robot lineup](https://navbot.com/collections/complete-robots), including:

    - [Kinatric D2](https://navbot.com/products/kinatric-d2): a quadruped robot.
    - [Kinatric D2W](https://navbot.com/products/kinatric-d2w): a wheel-legged robot.
    - STEM robotics kits and desktop robots for hands-on learning.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your configuration">
    Open your product's page to check its specifications, available configurations, and included hardware. Use the information for your exact model when planning software, accessories, or development work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Talk to the NavBot team">
    Email [sales@navbot.com](mailto:sales@navbot.com) for product selection, project inquiries, or technical support.

    To help us respond, include:

    - The robot model or product you are interested in.
    - Your intended use, such as teaching, research, or prototyping.
    - Your order number, if you already own a NavBot product.
    - A short description of your question, including any relevant error messages.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Need model-specific setup instructions or software resources? Contact
  [sales@navbot.com](mailto:sales@navbot.com) and tell us which product and
  configuration you use.
</Note>
